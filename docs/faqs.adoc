= Allowance FAQs

The current FAQs are available inside Allowance, beneath Usage Guide and above API Info. This guide summarises the same payment and operator behavior.

== Failed payments and retries

Before submission, failures retry after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 minutes, then hourly. Automatic retries stop after 24 hours or at the next regular scheduled time, whichever comes first. A full window allows roughly 29 attempts including the initial attempt; request duration can reduce this. Pending or uncertain submitted payments are checked rather than automatically sent again.

Schedules do not replay every missed interval. An overdue allowance may make one overdue payment and then advance to its next future anchored date. Several overdue allowances can each attempt a payment; there is no extension-wide combined spending cap. Wallet and server limits still apply.

== Failed versus skipped

Failed means the occurrence did not complete successfully. Skipped means it was deliberately passed over, for example during recovery without catch-up. An occurrence can fail first and then have its remaining retries skipped. Skipping sends no funds.

In Payment history, open Details. Eligible failed entries offer Retry this payment; eligible skipped entries offer Pay this skipped occurrence. Confirm the original date, recipient and amount. Only that occurrence is queued and the regular schedule is preserved. Older occurrences require saved details that still match; paused/ended allowances, submitted payments and conflicting queued or due payments prevent the action. The UI explains the reason.

Payments sent separately from a wallet appear in wallet history, but are not automatically linked to an allowance. Check wallet history before requesting an occurrence payment you may already have sent.

== Networking

Recipients normally require public HTTPS endpoints on port 443. With split DNS, a hostname may resolve to a private IP inside LNbits. The operator can configure exact trusted hostname/private IPv4 pairs through `ALLOWANCE_TRUSTED_LNURL_DESTINATIONS`; see link:../README.md#lnurl-network-policy[network policy]. Container recreation is required to change its environment. HTTPS validation and independent callback checks remain in place.

== Dates, currencies and history

Schedules are anchored to the original start and configured timezone; the browser displays datetimes in local time. Start, frequency and timezone are fixed after creation. Supported frequencies are minutely, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly.

Fiat uses LNbits' configured rate providers at attempt time. A later retry can therefore use a different sat amount. Choose sats for a fixed sat amount.

History shows the current occurrence and up to 50 completed entries. Up to 200 diagnostic events per allowance are retained. Older releases may not have saved all amounts or attempt details; Not recorded does not mean zero. Refresh and Check payment status never request a new payment.
