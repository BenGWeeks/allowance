= Troubleshooting Allowance

== Payments are not arriving

Check that the extension and allowance are active, the start date has arrived, the end date has not passed, and the source wallet has sufficient funds. Open Payment history → Details for the scheduled occurrence. Inspect its status, attempt events and retry eligibility reason. A future scheduled entry is not a payment attempt.

Failures before submission retry with bounded backoff. Pending or uncertain submitted payments require reconciliation, not an immediate resend. See link:faqs.adoc[FAQs]. The health endpoint `/allowance/api/v1/health` accepts the source wallet's invoice key and reports scheduler health, overdue and pending counts.

== Private-address error

A legitimate hostname may resolve to a private address inside the container because of split DNS. Repeated retries cannot change the policy. Ask the operator to inspect DNS from that container and configure only exact trusted hostname/private IPv4 pairs. See link:../README.md#lnurl-network-policy[network policy]. HTTPS certificates must remain valid; callbacks are validated independently.

== Missing or old history

Hard-refresh the browser after an upgrade. History retains the current occurrence and up to 50 completed entries; diagnostic logs retain up to 200 events per allowance. Older releases may not have saved amounts, recipient snapshots or detailed attempt events. Missing records cannot be reconstructed from the current schedule, and Not recorded does not mean zero.

A skipped occurrence may have failed earlier and then been deliberately passed over during recovery. Eligible skipped entries offer Pay this skipped occurrence; older entries without a verifiable unchanged snapshot cannot be paid through that action. Separate wallet payments are not automatically linked into Allowance history.

== Disabled payment actions

Read the explanation in Payment details. Typical reasons include an inactive or expired allowance, pending settlement, another queued or due payment, a submitted historical payment hash, or missing/changed saved details. Do not clear payment identities or edit the database to bypass these protections. Check wallet history before requesting payment if it may already have been sent separately.

== Upgrade or migration failure

Back up data and installed extension files before repairs. Deactivate the extension and restart LNbits before installing a repaired release; restart again afterward. Wait for LNbits startup migrations to finish before invoking another installer. Do not delete the database to resolve a duplicate-column error. The withdrawn 1.1.0 release needs the recovery procedure in link:../README.md[the README].

== Server logs and bug reports

Use `docker logs --since 30m <lnbits-container>` to inspect the relevant period. Report the Allowance and LNbits versions, timestamp, status/code from Payment details, and steps to reproduce. Redact API keys, credentials, recipient addresses, invoices and private infrastructure details before posting logs or screenshots publicly.
