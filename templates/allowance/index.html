Setup automated recurring payments to any Lightning address or LNURL-pay endpoint. Choose from flexible payment schedules (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) and pay in Bitcoin or fiat currencies with automatic conversion. Perfect for allowances, pocket money, subscriptions, and regular transfers.

How to use Allowances:

  1. Create a new allowance by clicking "New Allowance"
  2. Set the recipient's Lightning address (looks like an email address, e.g. user@getalby.com, but it's for Bitcoin payments not email)
  3. Configure the amount and currency
  4. Choose the payment frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
  5. Set start and end dates for the allowance
  6. Save and activate the allowance

Allowances will automatically send payments according to your schedule. You can pause, edit, or delete them at any time.
